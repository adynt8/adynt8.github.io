How to measure

Use the same technique researchers use so your numbers are comparable to the studies.

You'll need a rigid ruler for length and a soft tape measure (or a piece of string and a ruler) for girth.

Erect length

  1. Stand up, with a full erection, penis pointing straight out (parallel to the floor).
  2. Place the ruler along the top side of the penis.
  3. Press the end of the ruler gently into the fat pad until it touches the pubic bone. This is called bone-pressed length.
  4. Read the length at the tip of the head.

Erect girth

  1. Wrap a soft tape measure around the middle of the shaft (or the thickest part, if you're sizing condoms).
  2. Keep it snug but not squeezing.
  3. Using string? Mark where it overlaps, then measure the string against a ruler.

Flaccid length

  1. Measure in a warm room — cold makes the penis temporarily shrink.
  2. Stand, let it hang naturally without stretching.
  3. Measure along the top from the pubic bone to the tip.

Flaccid girth

  1. In the same warm, relaxed state, wrap the tape around the middle of the shaft.
  2. Snug, not tight. Read where it overlaps.

Getting a reliable number

  • Measure three times on different days and use the average. Erections and flaccid size vary with temperature, arousal and time of day.
  • Round to the nearest 0.25 cm or ⅛ inch — more precision than that is noise.
  • Don't measure along the underside or the side; that isn't how studies measure.
  • Log your measurements in the progress tracker to see the average over time.
