Privacy in plain English

  • Calculations happen in your browser. Your measurements are saved in your browser's local storage so they're there next time.
  • Sharing is opt-in. If you choose to contribute, we store your four measurements, a random ID and a timestamp. No name, email, IP-based location or account. Individual entries are never displayed publicly.
  • Photos never leave your device. The photo measuring tool works entirely in your browser.
  • Share links contain the numbers themselves. Anyone with a link you share can see those measurements — nothing else.
  • Analytics and ads. We use Google Analytics to count visits and Google AdSense to keep the site free. These may set cookies; see how Google uses data.
