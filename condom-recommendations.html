Tips

  • A condom should feel snug but not tight. Too loose risks slipping; too tight risks breaking and discomfort.
  • Brands that sell many exact sizes (such as MyONE Perfect Fit, Mister Size or TheyFit) let you fine-tune fit.
  • Example products are typical of each tier — widths vary by market and change over time, so always check the packaging.
  • Store condoms away from heat, check the expiry date, and only use water- or silicone-based lube with latex.
