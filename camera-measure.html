Measure from a photo

Use a bank card as a scale reference. The photo is processed only in your browser and never uploaded.

For an accurate result, the card and the penis must be in the same photo, at the same distance from the camera, with the camera pointing straight at them (not at an angle).
  1. Choose a photo that includes a standard bank/ID card (85.6 × 54 mm).
  2. Mark the card: drag a line along the card's long edge.
  3. Mark the length: drag a line along the top, from the base at the pubic bone to the tip.

Measured length

Accuracy depends on camera angle and how precisely the lines are placed — expect ±0.5 cm.
