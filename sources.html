Sources

This page lists the sources used for the average penis size statistics.

Population Average Data Sources

  • Global Average:
    • Veale D, et al. Am I normal? A systematic review and construction of nomograms for flaccid and erect penis length and circumference in up to 15,521 men. BJU Int. 2015
      • Average Erect Length: 13.12 cm (±2.66)
      • Average Flaccid Length: 9.16 cm (±1.57)
      • Average Erect Girth: 11.66 cm (±1.10)
      • Average Flaccid Girth: 9.31 cm (±0.90)
  • Western Average:
    • Herbenick D, et al. Erect Penile Length and Circumference Dimensions of 1,661 Sexually Active Men in the United States. J Sex Med. 2014
      • Average Erect Length: 14.0 cm (±2.7)
      • Average Flaccid Length: 9.5 cm (±1.6)
      • Average Erect Girth: 12.0 cm (±1.2)
      • Average Flaccid Girth: 9.5 cm (±1.0)
  • Asian Average:
    • Prospective investigation of penile length with newborn male circumcision and second to fourth digit ratio
      • Average Erect Length: 13.0 cm (±1.8)
      • Average Flaccid Length: 8.2 cm (±1.1)
      • Average Erect Girth: 11.5 cm (±1.0)
      • Average Flaccid Girth: 8.5 cm (±0.9)
  • African Average:
    • Wessells H, et al. Penile length in the flaccid and erect states: guidelines for penile augmentation. J Urol. 1996
      • Average Erect Length: 14.5 cm (±2.8)
      • Average Flaccid Length: 10.0 cm (±1.8)
      • Average Erect Girth: 12.5 cm (±1.3)
      • Average Flaccid Girth: 10.0 cm (±1.1)
  • European Average:
    • Prause N, et al. Standardized and Objective Measurement of Penis Size and Shape. J Sex Marital Ther. 2021
      • Average Erect Length: 14.3 cm (±2.7)
      • Average Flaccid Length: 9.7 cm (±1.8)
      • Average Erect Girth: 12.2 cm (±1.3)
      • Average Flaccid Girth: 9.6 cm (±1.1)
  • Latin American Average:
    • Moreno-Sepulveda J, et al. Penile Anthropometry in Brazilian and Colombian Men: A Cross-Sectional Study. J Sex Med. 2015
      • Average Erect Length: 13.6 cm (±2.2)
      • Average Flaccid Length: 9.1 cm (±1.5)
      • Average Erect Girth: 11.8 cm (±1.1)
      • Average Flaccid Girth: 9.3 cm (±0.9)
