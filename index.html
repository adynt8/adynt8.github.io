Contribute to the community stats?

You can anonymously add your measurements to the PlsSize.Me community dataset. It helps others see a broader picture of normal.

  • Only the four measurements and a random ID are sent. No name, email or location.
  • Your individual entry is never shown publicly. Only totals and averages are.
  • You can delete it any time from Your data.
